1. Introduction

Messages – SMS Texting App (“Messages,” “we,” “our,” or “the app”) is an Android messaging application that helps you view and manage SMS conversations, contacts, notifications, and message categories. This Privacy Policy describes the information the app may access and how it is handled.

By using the app, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. If you have questions, contact us using the email address above.

2. Information and Permissions

The information available to the app depends on the Android permissions you grant, the app's default SMS role, your device, and the features you use.

SMS and MMS messages

If you grant the required SMS permissions and, where necessary, set Messages as your default SMS app, the app may access message content and related information such as sender and recipient phone numbers, message dates and times, conversation/thread identifiers, and message status. This access supports displaying conversations, sending and receiving messages, unread counts, search, and message management.

The app may keep an app-managed local copy of message and conversation information in its Room database to support faster loading, search, categorization, and synchronization. The Android SMS Provider remains subject to Android's access rules and default SMS role requirements. Some actions, including sending or deleting SMS messages, may only be available while the app has the required permissions and role.

Contacts

If you grant Contacts permission, the app may read contact names, phone numbers, and related contact information so it can identify conversation participants and display contact details. Contact information is used for messaging features and may be cached locally to improve performance. The app does not need Contacts permission to display a phone number that is already present in a message.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission and enable notifications, the app may display notifications for incoming messages. Notifications may include the sender's name or number, message preview, and time. You can control notification permission through Android settings and available in-app notification preferences. Lock-screen visibility also depends on your device's notification and privacy settings.

Default SMS app role

You may choose to set Messages as your default SMS application through Android's system role dialog. This role enables certain SMS functions and does not, by itself, mean that every runtime permission has been granted. SMS, Contacts, and notification permissions are checked separately. You can change your default SMS application in Android settings.

Message categorization and app features

The app may classify messages into categories such as Personal, OTP, Finance, and Offers using message content, sender information, and locally implemented classification rules. This processing supports filtering and organization. Search indexes, favourites, archive status, spam status, and other app preferences may also be stored locally. Message categorization may not always be accurate; please review important messages yourself.

Recycle Bin and deleted messages

If the app provides a Recycle Bin, a copy of a message that you delete may be retained locally for a limited period (currently intended to be 30 days) to support restoration. Actual deletion from or restoration to the Android SMS Provider depends on Android permissions, default SMS role, and system support. You can permanently remove items using the available controls. Do not assume that uninstalling the app also removes messages stored by Android or restores messages held in the Recycle Bin.

Device and app information

The app and its third-party SDKs may process technical information such as app version, device model, operating system version, diagnostics, performance information, and identifiers required for app functionality, security, analytics, or advertising.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed by the app may be used to:

  • Display, organize, search, send, and receive SMS conversations where supported.
  • Match phone numbers with names in your contacts.
  • Show message notifications and unread status.
  • Classify and filter messages into categories.
  • Provide favourites, archive, spam-management, and Recycle Bin features.
  • Store app preferences and improve performance and reliability.
  • Display and measure advertisements through third-party advertising services.
  • Respond to support requests that you send to us.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

Core messaging data and app-managed message information are intended to be processed and stored locally on your device. The app may store message records, conversation summaries, contact display information, category labels, favourites, archive/spam status, preferences, and Recycle Bin copies in its local database or app storage.

We do not intentionally transmit the contents of your SMS messages or your contact list to servers operated by us for the core messaging, search, or categorization features. This statement does not cover information processed by third-party services such as advertising SDKs, or any separate feature that clearly explains that it sends information elsewhere. Review the permissions and disclosures presented by the app before enabling optional features.

We do not sell your personal information. Third-party service providers may collect or process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The app may use Google AdMob to display advertisements, including native ads. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location signals, app interactions, diagnostics, and ad performance information to provide, personalize where permitted, measure, and protect advertising services. The information processed can depend on your region, consent choices, device settings, and the SDK configuration.

Where required, the app may present consent choices before requesting ads. You can also review relevant settings on your device or Google account.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored app data remains on your device until you remove it using app controls, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup and device settings. Recycle Bin items may be retained for up to 30 days if that feature is enabled. Messages stored in the Android SMS Provider are managed under Android's system rules and may remain independently of the app's local cache.

You can manage or delete messages using the app where supported. Deleting an item from the app may require the app to be the default SMS application. If an operation cannot be completed because Android denies access, the app should indicate that it was not completed.

Third-party providers determine their own retention practices for information they receive. We do not control their retention periods.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app. Locally stored information is subject to your device's security, Android protections, and your device settings. Information transmitted by third-party SDKs is handled according to the providers' security practices. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Controls

  • SMS permissions and default app: Manage SMS permissions and your default SMS application through Android settings. Some messaging features may not work without the required access or role.
  • Contacts: Grant or revoke Contacts permission through Android settings. Without it, conversations may show phone numbers instead of saved contact names.
  • Notifications: Manage notification permission, notification channels, lock-screen visibility, and in-app notification preferences.
  • Local messages and app data: Use available app controls to manage messages, favourites, archive, spam, and Recycle Bin items. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may remove locally stored app information.
  • Advertising: Use available consent choices and device or Google account settings to manage advertising preferences.
  • Support: Contact us using the email address at the top of this policy with privacy questions or requests concerning information we control.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is a general-purpose messaging utility and is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through servers operated by us. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support channel, contact us so we can review the request.
